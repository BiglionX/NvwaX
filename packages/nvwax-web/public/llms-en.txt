# NvwaX

> NvwaX is an AI Virtual Company Factory platform: it assembles AI teams (AiTeams) from conversational requirement analysis, offers 240+ searchable AI Agents, reusable team collaboration templates (Team Skills), and a bounty marketplace (Bounties) — helping individuals and businesses run real work with AI agents.

## About NvwaX

NvwaX (Nüwa X) is named after the Chinese myth of "Nüwa creating humans", symbolizing the creation of your virtual company out of AI agents. Core capabilities:

- **AI Agent Search**: 240+ AI agents across R&D, content, marketing, data analysis, and more
- **AiTeam Assembly**: describe your needs in natural language and get a full AI team configuration with a leader and members
- **Team Skills**: reusable team collaboration templates (roles, workflows, collaboration rules) applied to projects in one click
- **Bounty Marketplace**: post tasks, find skills, and earn rewards
- **Developer Platform**: REST API, Webhooks, SDK, and desktop integration with ProClaw
